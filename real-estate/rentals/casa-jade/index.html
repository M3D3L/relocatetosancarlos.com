Casa Jade

5 Bed 4.5 Bath

Luxurious 5-Bedroom Retreat in Country Club

Experience the ultimate in comfort and style at Casa Jade, a stunning rental property featuring 5 spacious bedrooms and 4.5 modern bathrooms. Perfect for families or groups, this home offers a heated pool and breathtaking views.

MXN 13,500 per night
Welcome to Casa Jade, your dream rental located in the prestigious Country Club area. This exquisite property boasts 5 generously sized bedrooms and 4.5 bathrooms, ensuring ample space for relaxation and privacy. The heart of the home is the inviting heated pool, perfect for year-round enjoyment, complemented by an outdoor shower for convenience. Enjoy spectacular sunsets from the rooftop sunset deck, where you can unwind and take in the stunning views of Cerro Tetakawi. Inside, the home is equipped with mini-split A/C units to keep you cool during warm days, and a smart TV for your entertainment needs. The built-in BBQ grill and outdoor seating area create an ideal setting for alfresco dining and gatherings with family and friends. Additionally, the large water cistern (pila) ensures a reliable water supply. Casa Jade is not just a rental; it's a lifestyle choice for those seeking luxury and comfort in a serene environment.
