Joyas del Mar – Seven-Condo Residential Complex

Fully operational seven-unit, two-story condominium complex currently generating rental income. Each unit features a ground floor with a fully equipped kitchen, living and dining areas, full bathroom, and private terrace with barbecue and pool views. The upper floor includes two bedrooms, a full bathroom, and a private terrace with ocean views. All units are fully furnished and equipped, including granite kitchens, appliances, mini-split A/C in all areas, TVs, ceiling fans, outdoor furniture, beds, and sofa beds—ready for immediate operation. The complex also offers a swimming pool and social area, reception and maintenance room, shared laundry center, storage room with hydropneumatic equipment, and individual electric meters for each unit.

MXN 31,900,000
Joyas del Mar is a luxurious seven-condo residential complex that offers an unparalleled living experience. Nestled in a prime location, this complex features a stunning swimming pool, perfect for relaxation and leisure. Each condo is equipped with high-speed Wi-Fi, ensuring you stay connected at all times. Enjoy the convenience of private parking and the comfort of your own private balcony or terrace, where you can soak in the breathtaking views of Cerro Tetakawi. The fully equipped kitchen allows for culinary creativity, while the in-unit washer and dryer add to the convenience of modern living. Each unit is fitted with mini-split A/C units for optimal climate control, and keyless smart locks provide enhanced security. The beautifully landscaped garden offers a serene escape, complemented by 24/7 security for peace of mind. An outdoor seating area is perfect for gatherings or enjoying the fresh air. Experience the perfect blend of luxury and comfort at Joyas del Mar.
